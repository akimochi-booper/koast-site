# Koast website source map

| Changing | Start here | Focused verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dated guide or article image | [Editorial workflow](EDITORIAL.md), `content/posts/<slug>.json`, `editorial.py` (`load_posts`), `generate.py` (`blog_page`, `post_card`). Editorial dates and image identity come from the post. | `python generate.py` followed by `python scripts/check_site.py --today YYYY-MM-DD`. Review the actual article, index card and call to action on desktop and mobile. |
| Website quote request and team receipt | `quote-request.js` (`createQuoteSubmission`) and `marketing-design.js` own the browser state. `workers/koast-chat/worker.js` routes `/quote-requests` to `quote-requests.mjs` (`handleQuoteRequests`, `quoteEmail`). | `node --test test/quote-submission.test.mjs workers/koast-chat/test/quote-requests.test.mjs`. The mocked tests exercise duplicate submissions, retries, provider failures and receipt handling. |
| Website build, indexing and Pages | `generate.py` (`enhance`, `_validate_indexing_contract`, `_write_redirects`), `scripts/check-pr.mjs`, `scripts/stage_pages.py`, `scripts/check_site.py`, [Site checks](.github/workflows/site-checks.yml). | `node scripts/check-pr.mjs`, `test/indexing-redirects.test.mjs` and `test/test_stage_pages.py`. Keep the required `Site PR validation` name. |
| Legal pages and accepted contract text | `content/legal/`, `generate.py`, `_headers`, `_redirects`. Keep contract bytes, clean URLs and existing public links stable. | `test/legal-pages.test.mjs` and `test/standing-authorization.test.mjs`. |
| Marketing layout and winery discovery | `src/marketing_design.py` composes the generated page. `src/wine_designs.py`, `src/wine_imagery.py`, `src/wine_planning.py`, `wine-controls.js` and `css/wine-reference.css` own the winery layouts, search and controls. General styling lives in `css/main.css` and `css/marketing-design.css`. | `test/marketing-design.test.mjs`, `test/winery-discovery.test.mjs`, `test/wine-quote.test.mjs` and [visual QA](design-qa.md). Preserve approved compositions, noindex previews and keyboard access. |
| Wine-day ideas | `src/wine_days.py` (`WINE_DAY_IDEAS`, `render_wine_days`), `wine-days.js` and `marketing-design.js`. Ideas carry into the enquiry review. Existing Moovs booking owns its separate handoff. | `test/wine-days.test.mjs` and [wine-day decisions](docs/wine-day-ideas.md). |
| Search landing pages | `src/palo_alto_car_service.py`, `healdsburg_wine_tours.py`, `src/livermore_wine_tours.py`, `src/sacramento_car_service.py` and `src/stags_leap_transport.py` supply the city or wine branch of `generate.py`. Keep group transport quote-only. | Matching city/region `test/*.test.mjs`, [search evidence](docs/) and desktop/mobile inspection. |
| Acquisition and attribution | `koast-acquisition.js` (`track`, `nativeBookingLink`, `handoff`), [data handling](README.md#acquisition-measurement), `content/legal/privacy.inc`. Book owns order attribution. | `test/acquisition.test.mjs`, `test/legal-pages.test.mjs`. |
| Approved first-party website to native journey | `koast-journey.js` (`createKoastJourney`) receives current tab/session/privacy from `koast-acquisition.js`. `marketing-design.js` emits fixed quote milestones; `generate.py` emits fixed assistant milestones and uses the explicitly approved `JOURNEY_MEASUREMENT_LIVE` rollout switch. The approved matching notice is in `content/legal/privacy.inc`; activation requires coordinated schema, dedicated signing secret and native integration. | `test/journey.test.mjs`, acquisition, quote and website-chat regressions; generated script parsing. No form/message contents, IDs, raw UA or persistent identity. |
| Website assistant, photos, voice and links | `generate.py` (`CHAT_WIDGET`, `enhance`), `src/chat-voice.mjs`, `koast-chat-photos.js`, `koast-chat-links.js`, `css/main.css` and the [chat Worker](workers/koast-chat/README.md). The chat uses public support facts; the quote-form endpoint has its own handler. | `test/website-chat*.test.mjs`, `test/chat-voice.test.mjs`, `test/website-support-controller.test.mjs`, Worker tests and [assistant QA](docs/website-assistant-design-qa.md). |
| Favicon, article and social previews | `scripts/build-favicon.mjs`, `img/koast-wordmark-orange-v1.svg` and `generate.py` (`_update_favicon_links`, `_social_image`). | `test/social-images.test.mjs`, [image provenance](docs/image-provenance.md) and [social-preview evidence](docs/social-preview-20261113.md). |

Run JavaScript tests listed by filename with `node --test <file>`. Run `python generate.py` first for tests that inspect generated pages. The full validator does this automatically. Dated source research, image provenance and release evidence are indexed in [the editorial workflow](EDITORIAL.md) and [docs](docs/).

`koast-acquisition.js` also prepares a ten-minute signed, one-use native handoff without delaying navigation. The Book client scrubs its fragment and validates the capability server-side. `test/journey-bridge.test.mjs` verifies privacy/failure/navigation boundaries. Typed contents and raw identifiers never enter interaction properties; exact account/order associations are separate authorized measurement links.
