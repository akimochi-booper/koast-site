# SJC and OAK landing-page refresh — October 13, 2026

Scope: airport content, search titles/descriptions, terminal guidance, pickup preparation and relevant existing route links. Uses the existing page design; no rates, allowances, booking behavior, tracking contract, shared styles or shared scripts changed.

## Why these pages

Search Console baseline recorded October 13 for July 12–October 11, 2026: SJC 905 impressions / 0 clicks; OAK 863 impressions / 0 clicks. Relevant property-level queries included OAK terminal 2 car service (146 impressions), OAK terminal 1 car service (127), and black car service San Jose (129). These queries are not claimed to be page-filtered. Page-specific average positions were not available in the recorded baseline, so low CTR cannot be attributed to snippets alone. These changes do not guarantee rankings or clicks.

## Changes

- Search titles and H1s explicitly identify San Jose / Oakland airport car service.
- SJC now has distinct Terminal A, Terminal B and international-arrival instructions from the airport's current limousine page. The previous generic curbside wording was inappropriate for these designated pickup locations.
- OAK gives separate Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 preparation and links official airport information. It does not invent a limousine curb number or equate general passenger approach lanes with permission to load anywhere.
- Both explain quote inputs, waiting and delayed-flight allowances, departures and relevant onward trips. Pricing caveats follow existing published Terms; no policy change.
- Existing travel-time estimates are preserved and explicitly labelled approximate. Existing route guides are linked; their older travel-time claims were outside this content pass.
- No meet-and-greet marketing, new fleet capacities, advertised prices, reviews or customer claims added.
